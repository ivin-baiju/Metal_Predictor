Section 01 · Architecture

Hybrid Ensemble Components

Temporal Fusion Transformer (TFT) 30–36% Weight

Multi-horizon attention architecture capturing regime shifts across US 10Y TIPS real yields, USD/INR FX transmission, and seasonal industrial demand cycles across 1D to 5Y horizons.

N-BEATS Neural Basis 18–30% Weight

Decomposes raw commodity spot series into interpretable trend and harmonic seasonality basis functions without requiring hand-crafted lag assumptions.

XGBoost & LightGBM Quantiles 20–35% Weight

Gradient-boosted quantile regressors estimate non-linear inventory thresholds (LME/SHFE warrants, COMEX registered stock) and generate the shaded 95% confidence interval bands.

VECM Cointegration + FinBERT NLP 18–22% Weight

Vector Error Correction Models anchor long-run COMEX/LME-to-MCX parity and Gold/Silver/Platinum cross-ratios while FinBERT scores real-time macro and supply dispatches.

Section 02 · Data & Parity Pipeline

Server-Side TTL Caching & Local Unit Conversion

To respect free market-data API limits and keep API credentials strictly off the browser, Metal Prediction batch-caches spot benchmarks and USD/INR on the Express backend via GET /api/prices (15-minute TTL). Domestic MCX equivalents and tax-paid landed parity are computed locally:

Precious Metals (Au · Ag · Pt)
Base Landed = (COMEX_USD_oz / 31.1035) × Grams × USD_INR
Tax-Paid Parity = Base Landed × (1 + Duty) × (1 + GST)
Base Metals (Cu · Al)
Base Landed = (LME_USD_MT / 1000) × USD_INR
Arbitrage Spread = MCX_Spot − Tax_Paid_Parity
