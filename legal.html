GDPR & India DPDP Act 2023 Aligned Section 02 · Data Governance

2. Privacy Policy

We process personal and technical data transparently in accordance with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

A. Categories of Data Collected

  • Contact & Feedback Data: Name, email address, topic, selected metal, and message content voluntarily submitted through the Support & Feedback form.
  • Watchlist & Functional Preferences: Selected metal, display currency (MCX INR / Global USD), and forecast horizon stored locally in browser storage when Functional preferences are enabled.
  • AI Query History & Session ID: Prompts sent to the local MetalNLP-v1 Copilot along with an ephemeral browser sessionStorage UUID and active chart context.
  • IP & Security Telemetry: Request IP addresses processed temporarily in memory for abuse prevention and rate limiting (15 requests/minute on /api/chat), plus aggregate performance metrics only if Analytics consent is enabled.

B. Third-Party Services & Data Retention

  • Self-Hosted AI & Static Infrastructure: Chat inference runs 100% locally on our self-hosted trained MetalNLP-v1 classifier without transmitting prompts to paid third-party LLM APIs. Static UI libraries and fonts load via Google Fonts, Tailwind CDN, jsDelivr, and unpkg.
  • Retention Windows: Chat session IDs live in ephemeral server memory and expire upon server restart or session end. Rate-limit IP windows auto-purge every 60 seconds. Support/feedback tickets are retained only as long as necessary to resolve your request (maximum 12 months).
  • No Sale of Personal Data: Metal Prediction never sells, rents, or brokers user emails, query histories, or watchlists to third-party advertisers.

C. Your Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR Arts. 15–21 & DPDP Act Secs. 11–14)

You have the right to access information about processed data, request correction or erasure of submitted support messages, withdraw non-essential consent at any time, nominate an authorized representative (under DPDP), and lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority or the Data Protection Board of India.
